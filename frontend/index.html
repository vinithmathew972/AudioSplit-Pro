WORKSTATION HELP & SHORTCUTS
WORKFLOW QUICKSTEPS:

1. Import audio or video (MP3, WAV, M4A, FLAC, MP4).

2. Review the live waveform, timecode ruler, and audio stream profile.

3. Select desired duration interval (5m, 10m, 15m, 20m, 30m, or Custom).

4. Click EXECUTE AUDIO SPLIT to slice without generational loss.

5. Download individual segments or complete ZIP bundle.

WORKSTATION SHORTCUTS:
Space Play / Pause Audio Preview
Enter Execute Audio Split (when in settings)
Esc Close Help / Modal
